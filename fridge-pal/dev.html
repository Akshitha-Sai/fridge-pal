My Inspiration

The inspiration for FridgePal came from a personal experience. Like many people, I often found myself throwing away food that had expired before I could use it. I'd buy groceries with good intentions, but life would get busy, and I'd forget what I had in the fridge.

"I realized this wasn’t just my problem — many households face the same issue. Wasting food means wasting time, effort, and valuable resources."

While researching, I found that households waste nearly 30% of the food they buy. This leads to unnecessary money loss and food ending up in landfills.

I wanted to create a solution that was:

  • Easy to use for everyone
  • Visually appealing and engaging
  • Actually helpful in reducing waste
  • Educational about food preservation
  • Connected to modern shopping habits

FridgePal is my attempt to solve this problem using technology that's accessible, intelligent, and genuinely helpful in our daily lives.

Special Features of FridgePal

FridgePal isn't just another food tracking app. Here are the special features that make it unique:

AI Chef Assistant

Get personalized recipe suggestions based on what's in your fridge. The AI learns your preferences and suggests creative ways to use ingredients before they expire.

Smart Barcode Scanner

Simply scan grocery items to automatically add them to your inventory with expiry dates and categories. No manual data entry needed!

Store Locator

Find nearby grocery stores with real-time inventory, compare prices, and get directions with estimated travel time.

Fridge Inventory

Add, view, and manage all your fridge items in one place. Track quantities and expiry dates so you always know what’s inside your fridge.

Interactive Recipe Book

A beautiful digital recipe book with search functionality, categorized recipes, and step-by-step cooking instructions.

Smart Notifications

Get timely reminders about expiring items with recipe suggestions to use them. Never waste food again!

What makes FridgePal truly special is its focus on prevention rather than just tracking. We don't just tell you what's expiring—we help you use it creatively!
